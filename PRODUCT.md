# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People managing asthma or respiratory health who want a low-friction, mindful way to track triggers and medication. They seek a tool that feels supportive and tactile rather than clinical.

## Product Purpose

To transform the ritual of asthma management from a medical chore into a mindful practice. Success is a tool that users *want* to open, where logging data feels rewarding and the visual state reflects their respiratory well-being.

## Positioning

A "tactile health journal" for respiratory health. Unlike clinical logs, it emphasizes the *feeling* of breathing and uses a "mood-tracker" aesthetic to make health data feel personal and approachable.

## Operating Context

Used daily (or during flares) to log medication doses and respiratory check-ins. Often used in moments of stress or recovery, requiring a calm, non-overwhelming interface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Log Doses:** Quick entry of medication and time.
- **Log Check-ins:** Tracking peak flow, customizable symptom presets, and activity presets.
- **Visualize:** High-polish trends and control scores.
- **Customization:** Ability to customize the presets for symptoms and activities to fit individual triggers.
- **Visual World:** Aligned with the "Pink Mood Tracker" aesthetic—soft, polished, and serene.

## Brand Commitments

- **Aesthetic:** Soft, serene, polished.
- **Metaphor:** Air, expansion, and contraction (subtle, not overwhelming).
- **Voice:** Supportive, calm, and mindful.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing `BreatheScreen` implementation providing the functional baseline.
- Bloom (Pink Mood Tracker) as the primary visual and customizability benchmark.

## Product Principles

1. **Breath as Rhythm:** The UI should mirror the natural rhythm of breathing—expanding and contracting subtly to create a sense of calm.
2. **Tactile Reward:** Every interaction (logging a dose, toggling a symptom) should feel tactile and rewarding, moving away from "form-filling."
3. **Personalized Truth:** The tool adapts to the user's specific triggers via customizable presets, making the data truly theirs.
4. **Serenity Over Clinicality:** Prioritize a soft, mood-tracker aesthetic over medical layouts to reduce the anxiety associated with health tracking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Focus on high contrast for critical data (Peak Flow) while maintaining a soft overall palette. Ensure all tactile elements remain accessible via keyboard/screen reader.
